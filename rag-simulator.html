Stage 01

Knowledge Source

RAG begins with information outside the language model. Think PDFs, policies, websites, manuals or internal documents.
Employee Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 24 paid leaves per year.

The annual entitlement consists of 12 casual leaves and 12 sick leaves.

Employees should submit leave requests at least 2 working days in advance.
Stage 02

Intelligent Chunking

The document is divided into smaller units so that the retrieval system can locate the most relevant information.

Employees are entitled to 24 paid leaves per year.
CHUNK 002
The annual entitlement consists of 12 casual leaves and 12 sick leaves.
CHUNK 003
Employees should submit leave requests at least 2 working days in advance.

BESPOKE EXPLANATION

Knowledge

What happens?
RAG begins with external knowledge. These documents contain information that the LLM may not have seen during training.
Why?
Your application may contain private, current or domain-specific information. RAG gives the model access to that information at query time.
Key concept
External Knowledge
Mental Model
Think of RAG as:

Search first. Answer second.

The LLM is not directly searching your documents. The retrieval system finds the useful information first. The LLM then uses that information to formulate the answer.
